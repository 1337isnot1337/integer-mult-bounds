# The inherited contract and the gap

Idle-axis hosting moves helper residual ownership from banks to pending idle
coordinates. Its proof relies on the parent invocation order, bridge actions,
level progression, terminal exchange and local programs remaining unchanged.
The added hosting audit rederives windows from the supplied schedule. That
checks placements relative to that schedule, but does not establish that the
schedule is the admitted parent's schedule.

Two concrete regressions expose the distinction. Deleting the terminal
exchange changes no paid local call or idle rank. Changing a bridge coefficient
to zero changes neither a hosting window's recorded pair touches nor its paid
profile. Thus the original hosting audit accepts these changes. Literal F2
replay of the eight scalar data columns rejects both. The actual pinned
generator makes neither change.

The guard requires the hosted bit schedule to equal the parent's schedule
after removing only the enumerated `idx` field from idle entries. It preserves
all other keys, coefficients and order. JSON comparisons preserve numeric types
as well as values. Invocation lists must retain their count, order and every
non-owner field, including stage, replica, pair, orientation and local hash.
All other template fields are equal except the explicitly allowed version,
live count, helper-route description and hosting metadata. The unchanged
ownership audit remains responsible for the new owners, live count, hosted
slots, remainders and paid profile.

The guard also replays the parent scalar schedule and its levels independently.
Every local stage starts at its required level; every bridge joins equally
advanced pairs with a coefficient of +1 or -1; idle ranks match their original
level increments; the terminal exchange occurs once, last, after all pairs
reach level eight. The resulting eight F2 columns equal the identity. Exact
parent binding additionally preserves the bridge signs, which F2 replay alone
cannot distinguish. A matching malformed parent/child pair is therefore not
accepted merely because their schedules compare equal.

Under the independently admitted parent and the original hosting-ownership
premises, these checks enforce the unchanged scalar and schedule premises used
by the hosting proof. They do not prove the inherited all-size compiler,
correlated dirty-bank, precision, or analytic interfaces.
