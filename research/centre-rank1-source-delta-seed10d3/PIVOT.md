# Discarded Cycle 11 frame-triple screen

The first refined idea was to share a common 6D core across triples of the eight selected 8D centre frames. Exact GF(2) elimination gives dimension 6 for all 56 triples. The selected centre carriers are distinct registers, however, and the frame intersection does not establish shared input data or fanout. The favorable `C6 + 3*C2` budget therefore lacked a valid shared-call contract. No circuit search or cost claim was made from it. The source-side rank-one delta task in `TASK.md` replaces that idea before implementation.
