## Failed attempts and limits

The original p48/P80 coefficient producer reached600 seconds. A bounded
diagnostic located its global exponential setup in encoded multiplication.
After the exact setup-arithmetic refinement, the catalogue checker completed,
but Python's diagnostic traceback thread stalled during shutdown. I sampled
that owned process, confirmed the diagnostic deadlock and terminated it.
I removed the diagnostic hook and obtained the clean319.063-second result.

One combined fresh producer-plus-consumer attempt also reached600 seconds.
I split registered verification at the existing explicit page boundary and
retain both costs. The first archive contained an extra macOS sidecar; the
strict loader rejected it. I replaced its container, preserving the complete
verified numerical packet and its hash.

I stopped the production-native constructor after tracing its prime-sized
initialization. The finite data tests use retained literal q2 children.
Their reduced Gaussian halos establish staged conformance; they do not
establish production locality or the production Fourier-error allowance.
Production-sized native/tensor execution, CRT conversion, complete integer
recovery and independent human or formal-kernel review remain open.
