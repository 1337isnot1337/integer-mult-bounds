# A required consumer of the five-readout kernel

Clear1nsight, with substantial OpenAI assistance. This is a source-bound
answer to the two conditional handoffs in [PR #3554](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3554).
It establishes an exact admission failure, an admitted family with a required
observable, and a specific physical interface mismatch. It does not supply an
actual paid caller, an advantage from the enlarged entrance, or a new bound.

## 1. Frozen contract and inherited premises

Work over $R=\mathbb Z[i]$, put $\pi=1+i$, and define

$$
E_9=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&\pi\end{pmatrix}^{\otimes9},
\qquad B_9=E_9R^{512}.
$$

All events below are zero-based indices in the unchanged 5,398-event PR #2586
WORD. Its bytes and NATIVE recipes, executable dependencies, and credited
proofs are authenticated in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json). They are already
retained on the pinned canonical main commit
`20ecf2891d80b5acf18d980b2164fb44ea7faa90`.

Write $A_H$ for the source's actual ideal map from the empty frame to frame
$H$. Precisely, this is the physical chart
$\Delta_H=\overline{\Gamma_H}=\Gamma_H^{-T}$, with the endpoint conventions
of the retained PR #3416 proof. It is not the bare frame matrix. The
`observable.py` receipt also calls this chart $J_H$. Set

$$
\begin{aligned}
F&=[256,128,64,32,16,8,4,2,1],\\
O&=[256,136,64,40,16,4,2,1],\\
O_0&=[257,129,65,33,16,9,5,2],\qquad P_0=[400].
\end{aligned}
$$

The proof uses two explicitly inherited results. PR #3382 proves that the
actual affine/quadratic Gaussian monomial alphabet, complete coordinate
native stages, and their inverses preserve $B_9$. A completed literal signed-floor pair
equals its ideal pair on that domain. PR #3416 binds all 2,070 native
occurrences to the phase-correct identity
$U=A_{\mathrm{new}}A_{\mathrm{old}}^{-1}$. Their proofs are retained inputs;
this package does not rerun their complete operator certificates. The new
literal controls separately execute the actual signed-floor arithmetic on
the two specified fixtures.

For the five first-collector source groups $G_j$, define the mathematical
readouts

$$
\rho_5(y)_j=\frac14\sum_{Y\in G_j}y_Y.
$$

The groups have sizes $3,7,7,11,24$ and partition the 52 sources of the
PR #3467 producer cone. Both Y91 and Y90 belong to its D27 group. The strong
raw-source domain is $S=(4B_9)^{52}$, inside that cone's correlated entrance
$L$. These are source domains, not claims that every element of $L$ executes
the omitted events of the whole word.

## 2. The original witness fails at an actual omitted read

PR #3467's coefficient slot 44 is the D27 zero-gap read at event 1817, with
next read 1819 and no intervening D27 transport. Its read frame is $O$.
The exact encoder $K$ sends $a_{44}=\delta_{15}$, all other coefficients zero,
to the raw sources

$$
Y91=p,\qquad Y90=-p,\qquad p=A_O^{-1}E_9\delta_{15},
$$

with every other selected Y zero. Since $\pi^4=-4$, the field
$E_9\delta_{15}$ equals $-4$ on the 32 addresses containing mask 15, and is
zero elsewhere. The authenticated K encoder and decoder check the complete
coefficient meaning and its inverse.

The independent literal implementation reconstructs the same $p$ without
importing that codec or its Gaussian implementation. It starts from this
profile and reverses Y91's actual native history at events
195, 992, 1133, 1238, 1397, and 1598. The two complete 512-entry arrays agree.
The canonical JSON digest of $p$ is

`2d5e9e5b5d2ef3cbee879749e9f67b3c78c20eef2edf8f0b7737a0a65230503b`.

It has 256 nonzero Gaussian coordinates, real/imaginary component height one,
belongs to $B_9$, and does not belong to $4B_9$. The latter is checked by exact
$E_9^{-1}$ decoding, not by a floating-point or Euclidean size test.

Now place $p,-p$ in the two actual initial Y banks and zero in every other
initial public bank. The literal whole-word interpreter completes all 1,099
events before event 1099, with no completed event output outside $B_9$.
Event 1099 is exactly

```text
["add", "D1", "Y90", "1/4", [381,241,2]]
```

At address 10, the physical donor Y90 is $-2+0i$. Its required real increment
is $-1/2$, which is not on the specified Gaussian integer grid. Thus this
particular element admitted by the restricted producer cone does not satisfy
the full word's exact fractional-ADD contract. The verifier requires this
exact first failure and its preceding successful boundary.

This is stronger than merely observing $p\notin4B_9$, which would only fail
a sufficient product-domain condition. It is still a statement about this
input, this grid, and this exact-literal contract. It does not classify all
correlated full-prefix inputs, rule out a proved rounded-error contract, or
establish a least sufficient rescaling. Later formulas for the unscaled pair
are ideal row identities, not a claim to have executed past its failure.

## 3. An actual required observable exists

The D27 transient displayed by PR #3554 has no direct observer between events
1817 and 1819. Event 1818 only transports Y91. All ADDs reading D27 as a source
occur at events 916, 2030, and 2034. The relevant actual consumer is instead
the asymmetric Y90 read into D1 and the later Q0/G0/X0 path.

Under the inherited phase-correct covariance, write a bank at frame $H$ as
$A_H$ times its normalised value. Native transports then preserve that value.
Propagating the pair $(p,-p)$ formally gives the following selected cuts.
Other terms are zero on this pair direction.

The late sign matters. `observable.py` implements a separate reverse adjoint
of the full word, including every sign, and checks all 104 entries of the
complete X0 Y row against PR #3462's existing $CA/2$ certificate. That
certificate has

$$
A_{\bullet,91}-A_{\bullet,90}=(-1,1,0,0,1,-1,1,1)^T,
\qquad C_{X0,\bullet}=(1,0,0,0,0,0,0,0),
$$

where $A$ and $C$ in this display are the certificate's query matrices, not
the physical charts $A_H$. The response is therefore $-1/2$ on the pair.
An explicit control omitting event 5086 gives $+1/2$ and fails the claimed
signed endpoint. This check corrected a preliminary scalar triage omission
before publication; it is not a correction to PR #3554.

The same reverse computation proves that the complete normalised final rows
of D1, Q0, and G0 equal their respective initial identity rows, and that the
eighth collector's C row is zero at erasure. These are algebraic statements
for arbitrary formal initial values under the covariance premise. Their
actual returned frames are those of the word. They do not establish literal
admission for arbitrary initial dirt, or return every bank to its empty frame.

X0 is an existing required output of the whole word, omitted from PR #3446's
projection. Its projection-to-full frame gap is [386] to $F$. PR #3469 already
supplies the corresponding rank-eight native-only adapter. This package
authenticates that adapter and the one X0 row; it does not recompute the
rank-eight completion theorem or the full 675-port recipient closure.

## 4. All-$B_9$ admitted family and nonfactorisation

**Theorem.** Assume the inherited native preservation and phase-correct
covariance above. For every $u\in B_9$, initialise
$Y91=4u$, $Y90=-4u$, and every other public bank to zero. The entire original
word executes exactly on the fixed Gaussian integer grid. Every completed
event state belongs to $B_9$, all eight private-C lifetimes erase at zero, and

$$
\rho_5(y)=0,\qquad X0_{\mathrm{final}}=-2A_Fu.
$$

**Proof.** Run one scalar symbol through all 5,398 actual events, beginning
with normalised Y91 coefficient $1$, Y90 coefficient $-1$, and all others
zero. Apply every ADD coefficient and sign exactly as a rational number;
native transports only update the authenticated frame. `primary.py` checks
that both every stored bank coefficient and every individual ADD product
belong to $\tfrac14\mathbb Z$. It also checks the full chronological frame
constraints, final frames, and eight zero erasures. The exhaustive counts
are

| Denominator | Updated bank coefficient after an event | Individual ADD product |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4,928 | 3,006 |
| 2 | 390 | 52 |
| 4 | 72 | 46 |

The first column of counts covers every event except the eight erasures;
the second covers all 3,104 ADDs. All 208 signs, 2,070 transports, eight
allocations, and eight erasures participate in this finite certificate.

After substituting the input symbol $4u$, every stored normalised coefficient
and every individual ADD product is an integer multiple of $u$. Inductively,
at its actual frame it therefore belongs to $B_9$. For an ADD, the common
frame makes both the increment and updated value Gaussian integral in that
module. For a native, the credited monomials preserve $B_9$, the literal
signed-floor pairs agree with the ideal pairs, and each complete coordinate
stage preserves $B_9$. Disjoint pairs within that stage read disjoint inputs;
no claim that updating only one pair preserves the whole-array module is needed.
The covariance identity supplies the next normalised state. Signs and owned
zero lifetimes preserve the induction. The checked final scalar coefficient
of X0 is $-1/2$, giving $-2A_Fu$.

Internal floor-halves need not be exact divisions individually. The preserved
objects here are the completed pairs and their states; equality of those
pairs follows from the inherited Gaussian parity lemma. No stronger claim
about the pair's temporary arithmetic is needed. Finally, Y91 and Y90 are
in the same source group and cancel in all five sums. This proves the theorem.

For $u\ne0$, invertibility of $A_F$ gives $-2A_Fu\ne0$. Comparing with the
all-zero input shows that **no function of $\rho_5$ alone**, even a nonlinear
one, can supply this required X0 endpoint on the admitted strong domain.
The comparison fixes every other initial public bank at zero, so it also
rules out recovering this endpoint from $\rho_5$ plus those fixed values.

For an independent finite positive control, the literal interpreter uses
$u=p$. It executes all 5,398 events, binds all 2,070 native occurrences,
compares 102,656 completed signed-floor/ideal pairs on 223 active native
calls, and evaluates the other 1,847 native calls by their exact all-zero
action. It checks all 3,104 ADDs, 208 signs, eight erasures, and 358 final
frames. Q0 finishes at zero. The 52 X banks and the two retained Y sources
are the 54 nonzero final banks.

The integrated verifier compares all 512 independently prepared entries of
$p$, all 9,216 Gaussian coordinates of 18 Q0/G0/X0 checkpoints with their
frames, and both 512-entry returned Y arrays against a separate oracle.
That oracle uses the authenticated K encoder, one forward scalar symbol,
and short actual chart paths rather than the literal interpreter's full bank
histories. Complete arrays are compared before their hashes are recorded.
The reverse X0 adjoint is a third, separately written check.

This family lies entirely in $S=(4B_9)^{52}$ and has zero relative class in
$L/S$. It therefore demonstrates no benefit from the enlarged entrance.
Nor does it contradict PR #3554, whose complete caller returns all 52
coefficient banks. Those retained outputs still carry information for later
computation. The obstruction is to access restricted to the five readouts.

## 5. Why the unchanged word is not the coefficient supplier

The actual word contains exactly 728 writes targeting Y banks. Every one is
a native transport. There is no ADD, sign, allocation, or cross-bank write
into a Y. Thus each in-word Y state depends only on its own initial raw Y.

Under a direct one-physical-bank-per-independent-coefficient identification,
even after relabelling, one nonzero initial coefficient can affect only one
Y bank through those operations. But the exact K column in Section 2 has
two nonzero Y outputs. The unchanged word therefore does not materialise K
under that identification. This proves a specific source-interface mismatch,
not a universal impossibility of a caller.

One may define virtual coefficients $a=K^{-1}y$ algebraically. PR #3554's
strict gather still requires access to those actual coefficient values, or
a separately implemented and priced fused read. Renaming raw Y values does
not provide that access. An external coupled initializer is also possible,
with its stated ownership, common-frame reads, restoration, and complete bill.

There is a second concrete boundary. All 104 actual initial Y frames are
empty, while all 104 actual final Y frames have dimension eight. PR #3554
returns its 52 coefficient fields in empty frames. An adapter or a changed
caller contract can reconcile the outputs, with its costs and required reads
included. The two contracts are not automatically interchangeable.

## 6. Result boundary

The first handoff is resolved to this scoped interface gap, without a paid
physical caller. The second has an actual required legal observable on a
strong-domain family, plus an exact failure of the original unscaled witness.
[COSTS.md](COSTS.md) records the local acquisition and return components and
the obligations that prevent treating them as a complete supplier saving.

The revision-6 family gate and inherited unchanged-profile stop remain in
force. No selected record, source word, native recipe, or historical evidence
is modified. A further construction round needs a named compatible caller
and a complete allowance, or a specifically changed admission/consumer
contract with that allowance. The present result alone does not justify one.
