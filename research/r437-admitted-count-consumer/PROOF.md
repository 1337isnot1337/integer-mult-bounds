The composition uses the original R437 checked_entry, not its sharp-stock clone.
The intended domain is the certified prime q=2^127−1, e=n=64, w=2, h=0,
L=(q²).bit_length()+1=255, I=64.bit_length()+1=8, and the actual compiled Q.
The compatible original root stock is K=9475*A, where
A=28416*|GL64(F_q)|*q^4096. D(64)=1, so original and sharp stock coincide here.
No new proof of 9475, primality, ordinal ordering or directory reserve is claimed.

The entry authenticates the exact source/Specifications/handle, field and
parameter widths before a continuation may encode anything. The continuation
constructs actual source Word/Ring arenas with these exact widths, the root
Program with no STEP, and the exact binary Count K. Its context carries the
same q, dimension and live codebook object. Full directory.Context construction
is not claimed: its high_count producer is not needed by this NODE prefix and
has its own expensive charged construction obligation. The admitted host
parameter certificate itself is not a materialized giant payload.

The retained #4794 batch adapter divides K by the exact same A; its fixed-context
accepted-domain proof gives unused9475. The literal NODE prefix normalizes and
validates its actual binary roles, invokes the actual incoming callback on the
empty root prefix, saves unused, and checks divisibility by stock_multiple9475.
It then consumes unused in original Count.product statements for leading and
fibre, and original multiply_atom_power using the same encoded modulus. With
before=gap=suffix=1 this yields leading9475 and fibre9475*(q²)^128. No payload is
read: execution stops immediately before the original peel.run statement.

The proof obligation is the actual parameter/representation/consumer join, not
repeating a structural quotient fixture. The original NODE statements and
save/count/commit methods are reused, while only its fresh_rows dependency is
replaced by the opt-in exact quotient. Program root width/unit, arena widths,
codebook identity and serialized Count are tied to the accepted continuation.
The package will require unchanged borrowed bytes/heads and unrelated dirty
Count/Word spectators, owned numeric restoration, and cleared newly allocated
record backing. Negative entry controls must not call the continuation.

All construction, Count encoding, quotient, callback, Count consumers and owned
cleanup require their own measured source actions. The giant q^(4096) constant
has about 520,193 bits and that many possible bit positions but q^4096 remainder
states. Its native-host arithmetic is not a unit-time bit operation. Record
meters and word meters do not price full fixed-tape control, compiler tables,
large-divisor arithmetic or allocation. The result must expose these limits;
no complete physical tariff, full Context/GL64 directory, returned payload,
supplier admission or kappa improvement follows. #4927 independently audits
