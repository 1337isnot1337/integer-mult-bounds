# Conditional outer saving 0.00231165 after the native width-five change

Subject to the native and analytic hypotheses retained from PR #4359,
the changed word and weak-separation compiler give

```
T(n) = O(n (log n)^(1-kappa)),
kappa = 0.00231165.
```

This increases the first verified continuation point `0.00230508` by
`0.00000657`, and the original #4359 target `0.0023` by `0.00001165`.
The additional gain uses a literal change to the native word: the
102 unnecessary sixth-axis helper topups are deleted. Their corresponding
source complements remain charged by the finite compiler.

## The complete chain

productive endpoints and the complete invoice. It authenticates the actual

chronology, and disjoint sixth axes. All other operations and the previous
conservative scalar allowance remain in the word.

The [weak compiler interface](../kappa-4359-weak-compiler/README.md) supplies
the explicit rank guard `2<=k_min<m` and `r_max<=k_min`. It retains the
original integer flow and denominator-cleared compiler implementations.
The generic equality-case graph checks exercise several generations and
paid calls on owned centers.

The [weighted exit-tail proof](../kappa-4359-weak-separation/PROOF.md)
supplies the new analytic induction. The old strict-separation proof is
not reused at the equality `r_max=k_min=5`. A positive mixed moment at
`c=0.00231166`, together with the weighted-tail estimate, certifies the
fixed finite uniform template

```

uniform saving U = 0.002311658.
```

The finite program is defined by the exact integer compiler at this rank.
Its word and its complete counts are not materialized. Every source array
receives its complementary coordinates as well as its declared mixed job.
The native deletion certificate checks that a full one-unit completion at
rank 57 still costs exactly 127,570 rank-one leaves for 2,242 productive
arrays, before and after deletion. The higher saving arises only after
the mixed hierarchy is compiled.

## Finite-template constants and conditional scope

The new uniform template requires its own scalar, movement, owned-space,
and precision constants. Apply the generic formulas in the pinned
`FINITE-TEMPLATE-CLOSURE.md` to this new `N` and its exact finite compiler
counts. Deleting calls does not increase the local scalar allowance, but
the compiled word's counts must still be computed from its new hierarchy.
No small numerical constant from the old template is substituted here.

The unchanged BIT supplier already admits saving `0.0027`, so BIT remains
above the chosen common saving. Its atom exponent and reserve bounds
remain unchanged. Positive gaps `1-epsilon=2e-9` and `1-beta=6e-9`
absorb the new fixed template constants after enlarging the eventual
input threshold. The outer parameter proof gives the other required
strict inequalities explicitly.

This remains conditional on the source-bound native contracts and the
inherited analytic decomposition, fine-prime padding, native movement,
fixed-tape execution, and exact recovery premises. It is not a formal
verification of those premises, an unconditional multiplication theorem,
or a claim of practical template sizes.
